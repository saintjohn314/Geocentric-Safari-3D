GEOCENTRIC SAFARI 3D
A take on the classic GeoSafari Trivia game from the 90's, yet this is on an Azimuthal Equidistant map, otherwise known as the geocentric model, or flat earth model.
HOW TO PLAY
1. Read the target country or territory on the LCD display screen.

2. Click and drag with your mouse, or press A / D keys to turn the Earth disc.

3. Use your mouse wheel to zoom in close (up to 3.2x) to locate small islands and countries!

4. Click directly on the target country on the map to record your answer.
